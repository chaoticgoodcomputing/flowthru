# Glossary split into /GLOSSARY.md, sectioned by Developer role

The contributor vocabulary lives in `/GLOSSARY.md` (separate from `/CONTRIBUTING.md`), organized into four sections keyed to the Developer roles — Flow, Catalog, Extension, Core — that progressively need more vocabulary. We chose this over inline-in-CONTRIBUTING because it keeps CONTRIBUTING focused on philosophy and decision rules, lets readers stop at their role's section without scrolling past terms they don't need, and satisfies the "shield end-users from FP" design value structurally rather than via per-entry hedging.
